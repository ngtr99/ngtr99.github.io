About Me

Hi, I'm Diana Trang — a passionate and versatile tech enthusiast pursuing studies in both Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at Evergreen Valley College (expected December 2026). I love bridging the gap between hardware and software, building everything from DC-DC buck-boost converters with ESP32 microcontrollers to full-stack web applications using the MERN stack.

On the hardware side, I enjoy working with power electronics, embedded systems, and IoT solutions — designing circuits, writing PWM control logic, and validating real-world performance. On the software side, I build responsive, data-driven web apps and have hands-on experience with Python machine learning models, RESTful APIs, and cloud deployment.

Whether I'm debugging a circuit on a breadboard or deploying an application on Vercel, I bring the same curiosity, attention to detail, and drive to every project I take on. I'm actively seeking an internship where I can apply this unique blend of hardware and software skills — whether that means designing embedded systems, building IoT pipelines, or developing full-stack solutions — and grow alongside a team that values innovation, creativity, and real-world impact.
